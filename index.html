About Us

Helping people and organizations find the right fit.

Our Mission

Founded by alumni of IIT Guwahati, Mentors Provider Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. aims to simplify the job search process and connect job seekers with employers. We empower individuals to find meaningful employment while enabling organizations to identify top talent efficiently.

Our Focus

Our core focus is faculty recruitment and talent connection, with an emphasis on experienced IITian and NITian faculty as well as motivated fresher faculty opportunities.
